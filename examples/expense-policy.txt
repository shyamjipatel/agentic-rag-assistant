Demonstration expense policy (fictional company)

Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business travel, accommodation,
and client meals. Upload an itemized receipt and a brief business reason to the
finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Your manager must approve the
claim before finance processes payment. Reimbursements are paid with the next
monthly payroll after approval.

Personal purchases and travel upgrades are not reimbursable. Obtain written
approval before booking any business trip that is expected to cost over $500.
