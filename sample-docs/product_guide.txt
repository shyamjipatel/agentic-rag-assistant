NEXUS AI - PRODUCT GUIDE

Nexus AI is an enterprise knowledge assistant developed by ACME Technologies.

Supported Documents

Nexus AI currently supports PDF and TXT documents. The maximum supported individual file size is 20 MB.

Document Processing

Uploaded documents are parsed into text and divided into smaller chunks. Each chunk is converted into a vector embedding and stored in the vector database.

Question Answering

When a user asks a question, Nexus AI retrieves the most relevant document chunks. The retrieved context is supplied to a large language model to generate the final answer.

Source Citations

Every document-based answer should include references to the source documents used to generate the response.

Security

Documents are isolated by workspace. Users from one workspace cannot retrieve documents belonging to another workspace.

Retention

Deleted documents and their associated vector embeddings are permanently removed within 24 hours.
