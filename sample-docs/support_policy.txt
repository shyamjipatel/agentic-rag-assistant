ACME TECHNOLOGIES - CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Support Plans

Standard Support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.

Premium Support provides 24x7 support for production incidents.

Severity Levels

Severity 1 represents a complete production outage or critical security incident.

Severity 2 represents major functionality being unavailable while the overall system remains operational.

Severity 3 represents minor functionality problems or general technical questions.

Response Times

Premium customers receive an initial response within:

Severity 1: 30 minutes
Severity 2: 2 hours
Severity 3: 8 business hours

Standard customers receive an initial response within one business day.

Escalation

Severity 1 incidents are automatically escalated to the on-call engineering team.
