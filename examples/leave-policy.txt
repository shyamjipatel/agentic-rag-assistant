Demonstration leave policy (fictional company)

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Submit a
vacation request to your manager at least two weeks before the planned time off.
The manager approves requests based on team coverage. Up to five unused days
may carry over into the next year and must be used before March 31.

For sick leave, notify your manager before your usual start time. A medical
certificate is required when an absence lasts longer than three consecutive days.
