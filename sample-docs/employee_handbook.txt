ACME TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team members must be available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and an appropriate working environment.

Equipment Policy

Full-time employees receive a laptop and accessories required for their role. Employees may claim up to INR 25,000 once every three years for approved home-office equipment.

Learning and Development

Each employee receives an annual learning budget of INR 40,000. The budget may be used for technical courses, professional certifications, books, and approved conferences.

Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 days of paid leave each calendar year. Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward to the following year.

Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are eligible for 20 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
